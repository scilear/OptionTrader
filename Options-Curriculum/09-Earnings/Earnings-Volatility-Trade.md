# Earnings Volatility Trade

Trading earnings around IV expansion and crush, without betting on direction.

## Pre-Earnings Setup

**7–3 days before:** IV spikes as the market prices uncertainty
- **Trade:** Sell premium (strangles, condors) to capture the spike
- **Edge:** IV compression from current elevated levels, theta decay

**3–1 day before:** IV tops out
- **Trade:** Close positions or reduce size before the event
- **Risk:** Directional skew becoming extreme; gamma risk rising

## Post-Earnings Setup

**Day after announcement:** IV crush (30–60% collapse)
- **Trade:** Buy cheap premium (long straddles, long calls/puts) *before* earnings if you expect surprise
- **Trade:** Sell residual IV decay (short strangles, condors) in the day after if position didn't gap

## Key Insight

Earnings volatility trades are *not* directional bets. They're volatility level trades. You win by predicting whether IV will be higher or lower at specific dates — not whether the stock goes up or down.
