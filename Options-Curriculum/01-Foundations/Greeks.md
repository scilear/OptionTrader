# Greeks

The Greeks are the partial derivatives of an option's price with respect to underlying price, time, volatility, and interest rates.

**The Big Four:**
- **Delta:** Change in option price per $1 move in underlying
- **Gamma:** Change in delta per $1 move
- **Theta:** Change in option price per day (time decay)
- **Vega:** Change in option price per 1% change in volatility

See [[Greeks-Overview]] for comprehensive treatment.
