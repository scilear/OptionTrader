# Implied vs Realized Volatility

This is another term for [[IV-vs-HV|Implied Volatility vs Historical Volatility]]. 

**Implied volatility (IV):** Market's forward-looking forecast embedded in option prices.

**Realized volatility (RV):** Actual moves that occurred in the past 20/30/60 days.

The gap between them is the **Volatility Risk Premium** — the structural edge options sellers harvest.

See [[IV-vs-HV]] for full treatment.
