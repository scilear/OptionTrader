# Everything You Need to Become a Successful Options Trader

This vault is a comprehensive, practical guide to mastering options trading. Our mission is to teach you how to identify high-probability opportunities in the volatility surface, execute disciplined entries and exits, manage position risk realistically, and build strategies that survive real market conditions. Every concept is grounded in tested frameworks, not theory alone.

## Your Learning Path

Follow this sequence regardless of experience level. Each module unlocks the next.

### Phase 1: Theory (Weeks 1–2)
**Master the fundamentals before any trade entry.**

→ [[MOC-Foundations]]

Topics: Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega), implied volatility, skew, bid/ask spreads, open interest, market structure, GEX regimes.

**Why first:** You cannot read a volatility surface, size risk, or choose structures without understanding what moves option price and delta.

---

### Phase 2: Screening & Setup Recognition (Weeks 2–3)
**Learn to spot high-probability opportunities before they fully develop.**

→ [[MOC-Finding-Opportunities]]

Topics: IV rank, setup recognition patterns, gamma walls, market regime classification, earnings calendar screening, ticker selection.

**Why second:** With Greek mastery, you can now evaluate when volatility is cheap or expensive, and when setups have favorable risk/reward.

---

### Phase 3: Entry & Exit Timing (Week 3–4)
**Know when to enter, how to enter, and when to exit.**

→ [[MOC-Entry-Exit]]

Topics: Entry confirmation signals, intraday timing, bid/ask execution, spreads vs. single legs, profit-taking rules, early exit discipline.

**Why third:** Setup recognition is worthless without execution discipline.

---

### Phase 4: Strategy Selection (Weeks 4–5)
**Map each opportunity to the right structure.**

→ [[MOC-Strategies]]

Includes: Set-and-forget (CSP, covered call, wheel), income spreads (condors, butterflies, calendars), 0DTE tactics, long-term structures.

**Why fourth:** By now you know what moves prices and when entries occur. Now match each market condition to the structure with best edge.

---

### Phase 5: Risk Management (Week 5)
**Before any real money.**

→ [[MOC-Risk-Management]]

Topics: Position sizing, max risk per trade, portfolio limits, stop-loss placement, drawdown tolerance, adjustment triggers.

**Why fifth:** Never skip this. Poor risk management eliminates every edge you've learned.

---

### Phase 6: Advanced (Ongoing)
**Portfolio-level thinking, regime overlay, seasonal edges.**

→ [[MOC-Advanced]]

Topics: Earnings edge, long-term structures, portfolio margin, multi-leg hedges, seasonal patterns.

**Why optional:** Master the core path first. Return here once you are consistently profitable.

## The Why Behind This Order

This curriculum is designed around **testable edge**, not abstract theory:

1. **Foundations first** → You learn what actually moves option prices
2. **Screening second** → You learn to spot when prices are dislocated
3. **Execution third** → You learn to capture the dislocation in real time
4. **Structures fourth** → You learn which structure amplifies the edge
5. **Risk fifth** → You survive long enough to compound gains
6. **Advanced sixth** → You optimize portfolio-level returns

> [!warning]
> Do not skip Phase 5 (Risk Management). It is the difference between a profitable trader and a blown-up account. Many traders learn Phases 1–4, make good trades, then lose everything because they size wrong. Size discipline compounds over years. Execution mistakes are one-off costs.
