# Everything You Need to Become a Successful Options Trader

This vault is a comprehensive, practical guide to mastering options trading. Our mission is to teach you how to identify high-probability opportunities in the volatility surface, execute disciplined entries and exits, manage position risk realistically, and build strategies that survive real market conditions. Every concept is grounded in tested frameworks, not theory alone.

## Start Here

If you're new to options, follow this path:

1. **Foundations** — Begin with [[MOC-Finding-Opportunities]] to understand how to spot mispricings and read volatility surfaces.
2. **Execution** — Move to [[MOC-Entry-Exit]] to learn when and how to pull the trigger on your trades.
3. **Strategies** — Study [[MOC-Strategies]] for signal-to-structure mapping: which alerts warrant which positions.
4. **Risk First** — Deep-dive [[MOC-Risk-Management]] before you commit real capital.
5. **Advanced** — Once profitable, explore [[MOC-Advanced]] for portfolio-level thinking and regime overlay.
6. **Reference** — Bookmark [[Glossary]] for definitions and formulas.
