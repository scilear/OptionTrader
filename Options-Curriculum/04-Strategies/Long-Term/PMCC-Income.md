# PMCC-Income

PMCC positioned as an annualized income generator, with focus on multi-year return projections and portfolio-level income contribution.

## Key Points

- **Income modeling**: Project Year 1, Year 2, Year 3 annualized income from repeated short call rolls
- **Capital efficiency**: Extremely low margin requirement (often <5% of notional)
- **Reinvestment**: Decide whether to pocket income or redeploy credits into additional positions

For detailed mechanics, Greeks management, adjustment rules, and specific position examples, see [[PMCC]].

## Extended Timeframe Strategy

Rather than view PMCC as a single position, think of it as a 3–5 year income stream on a single underlying, with planned capital redeployment at key milestones.
