# LEAPS-Investing

LEAPS used as long-term stock substitutes or leveraged portfolio positions. See [[LEAPS]] for the core strategy mechanics and trade structures.

## Key Points

- **Capital efficiency**: Control large stock exposure with 1/10th the capital
- **Decay management**: Monitor time decay and theta bleed as expiration approaches
- **Rolling mechanics**: Plan multi-year laddered rolls or single-event transitions to stock

For detailed trade examples, position sizing, and risk management, see [[LEAPS]].
