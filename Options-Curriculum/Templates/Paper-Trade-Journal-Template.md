# Paper Trade Journal — <% tp.date.now("MMMM YYYY") %>

> [!warning]
> **Paper Trading ≠ Live Trading**
> 
> Results from paper trading can diverge significantly from live execution due to: (1) **Fill Quality** — simulated fills assume best-bid/ask, while live fills often worse; (2) **Emotional Detachment** — no real capital at risk changes decision-making; (3) **Position Sizing Discipline** — easy to oversize on paper without risk management pressure. Treat paper results as a learning tool, not a performance benchmark.
