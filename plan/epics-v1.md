# Epics (v1)

## EPIC-01 Ingestion & Data Quality
Goal: ingest option chains into snapshots with QC flags and deterministic storage.

## EPIC-02 IV Solve & Metrics
Goal: compute IV points (mid/bid/ask), delta grid, RR/Fly/Term metrics, z-scores.

## EPIC-03 Alert Engine
Goal: apply tiered IV validity, persistence, pessimistic gate, regime filter, explainability.

## EPIC-04 Trade Idea Generator
Goal: map alerts to Phase 1 structures with pricing bounds, greeks, risk labels.

## EPIC-05 Streamlit UI
Goal: alerts dashboard, metrics explorer, alert detail with confidence tier.

## EPIC-06 Replay/Backtest
Goal: deterministic recompute and feature-level event study.

## EPIC-07 Acceptance & Regression
Goal: implement acceptance tests and regression guardrails.
